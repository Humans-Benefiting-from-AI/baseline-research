# Weekly Newsletter Infrastructure: Setup & Deployment

## Overview

The "AI + Judaism Weekly" newsletter analyzes one Jewish AI tool per week from the 110-tool Registry backlog. This document covers setup, templates, and publishing workflow.

## Newsletter Goals

- **Audience:** Tech-aware Jewish communities, educators, institutional decision-makers
- **Frequency:** Weekly (Thursdays 8am EST recommended)
- **Length:** ~1,000-1,500 words per issue
- **Format:** Deep-dive analysis (5-10 min read)
- **Call-to-action:** Registry link + follow-up survey

---

## Platform Setup: Substack (Recommended)

### Why Substack?
- Free (take 10% commission, or pay Substack for full control)
- Built-in email + web publishing
- Analytics included
- Easy to grow subscriber list
- Integrates with social sharing

### Step 1: Create Substack Publication

1. Go to [substack.com](https://substack.com)
2. Click "Start a Publication"
3. Fill in:
   - **Name:** "Baseline: AI + Judaism"
   - **Tagline:** "Weekly deep-dives into Jewish AI tools and reasoning"
   - **Email:** Your address (subscribers reply here)
   - **Domain:** Custom domain optional (e.g., `baseline.substack.com` → `newsletter.baseline-research.io`)

### Step 2: Publication Settings

- **Monetization:** Free (no paywall; consider "free tier + paid optional" later)
- **Homepage:** Link to main Registry site
- **Navigation:** Add links to Registry, Whitepaper, About
- **Social:** Connect Twitter, LinkedIn for auto-sharing

### Step 3: Design & Branding

Create a simple template:

```
─────────────────────────────────
Baseline: AI + Judaism
Week NNN | YYYY-MM-DD
─────────────────────────────────

[TOOL NAME & SCREENSHOT/LINK]

[ANALYSIS SECTION 1: What it does]
[ANALYSIS SECTION 2: Strengths & Weaknesses]
[ANALYSIS SECTION 3: Halakhic implications or use-case]
[ANALYSIS SECTION 4: Comparison to alternatives]

─────────────────────────────────

Questions? Reply to this email or visit registry.baseline-research.io

Next week: [TOOL NAME]

─────────────────────────────────
Baseline Research • Independent • Empirical • Judgment-Based
```

## Writing Workflow

### The Editorial Calendar

Each week, pick one tool from the Registry backlog and publish a deep-dive:

| Week | Tool | Author | Status |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Sefaria | Elie | Publish |
| 2 | OpenAI's rabbi.ai | Elie | Publish |
| 3 | TBD | Elie | Draft |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

### Template: Weekly Analysis Outline

```markdown
# [TOOL NAME]: [One-Line Thesis]

## What It Does
[2-3 sentences on the tool's core feature]

## Technical Architecture
[How does it work? API? Hosted? Open source?]

## Strengths
- [Specific capability 1]
- [Specific capability 2]
- [What differentiates it from alternatives]

## Weaknesses
- [Limitation or bias]
- [Missing feature or design flaw]
- [Why it might fail for certain use cases]

## The Halakhic/Theological Question
[What is the interesting question this tool raises for Jewish practice?]

- Example: Does Sefaria's algorithm-driven sourcing change what counts as "authoritative" in halakhic reasoning?
- Or: What happens when a tool over-simplifies Talmudic reasoning to fit an app interface?

## Should You Use It?
[Honest recommendation: yes, no, or conditional]

## Next Week's Tool
[Preview the next analysis]

---

*Baseline Research reviews tools from the Registry weekly. Questions? [Email]*
```

## Building Your Subscriber Base

### Launch Strategy

1. **Week 0:** Publish 3 sample analyses before launch
2. **Announce on social:** "Baseline is starting a weekly newsletter. Subscribe now."
3. **Email networks:** Send personal invites to:
   - Registry users
   - Survey respondents (later)
   - Jewish tech communities
   - AI safety researchers

### Growth Tactics

- **Cross-promote:** Link from Registry homepage
- **Twitter thread:** Publish as both email + Twitter thread
- **Guest insights:** Invite Talmudic scholars or technologists to contribute occasionally
- **Community:** Build a Slack or Discord for subscribers to discuss tools

## Future Monetization (Phase 4+)

Once you have 500+ engaged subscribers:

1. **Paid tier:** "Baseline Pro" ($5/month) for exclusive deep-dives or analysis
2. **Sponsorship:** Let Registry tool creators sponsor their own analysis (with disclosure)
3. **Consulting:** Use newsletter credibility to pitch AI audit services to foundations

Track:

- **Replies:** Respond to every thoughtful comment/question

## Content Backlog (110 Tools)

The Registry contains 110 tools. At 1 per week, that's ~2 years of content. Prioritize:

**Month 1:** Flagship tools (Sefaria, OpenAI plugins, major platforms)
**Month 2:** Niche tools (specialized learning, compliance tools)
**Month 3+:** Emerging tools + repeats (annual "State of" pieces)
