## The Independence Constraint (125-150 words)
[TODO - ELIE: Explain the core thesis—why does independence matter? What does it enable?]

This is the heart of the manifesto. Explain:
- We are not answerable to any rabbinic authority, institution, or vendor
- This is not a limitation—it is the precondition for credible evaluation
- An auditor accountable to institutions cannot audit institutions
- A researcher answerable to the surveyed population is not doing research
- A reviewer with institutional relationships cannot review neutrally

[TODO - ELIE: Show the four strengths that flow from independence. Pick language that resonates.]

From the Independence Constraint doc:
1. **Empirical authority** — We measure what nobody else measured (Orthodox AI adoption survey, HRE benchmark). Publication = standing.
