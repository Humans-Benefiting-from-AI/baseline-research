This kit contains the exact copy you need to distribute the survey across your networks (WhatsApp, Email, LinkedIn) and the clean text block to copy-paste into your survey builder (Typeform, Qualtrics, or Google Forms).

## 1. Distribution Copy

### LinkedIn / Newsletter (The "Manifesto" Post)
**Headline:** Institutions buy comfort. We are measuring reality.
**Body:**
For the past year, the conversation around AI in the Orthodox Jewish community has been dominated by institutional anxiety and vendor hype. 

What we don't have is a baseline. Nobody actually knows how many rebbeim are using Claude to build shiurim, how many day schools have silent AI policies, or how many laypeople are asking ChatGPT halachic questions and acting on the answers.

Baseline Research is launching the first empirical, anonymous, longitudinal study of AI adoption in the Orthodox world. We aren't selling policy software, and we don't answer to a rabbinic board. We are just measuring what is actually happening.

If you are an institutional leader, an educator, or just a consumer of Jewish tech, take 3 minutes to put your reality on the record.
